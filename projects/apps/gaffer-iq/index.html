    Finding your FPL Team ID

    1. Sign in at fantasy.premierleague.com and open My Team.
    2. Open DevTools — right-click the page and choose Inspect, or press F12 (⌥⌘I on Mac).
    3. Switch to the Network tab.
    4. Reload the page (F5) with DevTools still open.
    5. Find the request named my-team/1234567/ — that number is your Team ID.

    Quicker route: from the Points or Transfers page the address bar reads /entry/1234567/event/5 — same number.
