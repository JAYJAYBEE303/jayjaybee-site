    How the score works

    Each side of a fixture gets six sub-scores from 0 to 100. The composite blends them into one number — higher means a kinder fixture for that side.

    1. Base difficulty — the opponent's overall strength, the starting point.
    2. Counter-matchup — this side's attack against that defence, and the reverse.
    3. Team form — recent results and underlying numbers, weighted to the last few games.
    4. Home/away split — how each side actually performs at this venue type.
    5. Style clash — whether the two styles of play suit or stifle each other.
    6. Head-to-head — recent meetings between the two clubs.

    The model isn't yet calibrated against live results — treat the score as structured context, not a forecast.
