--- layout: wiki-entry parent: fc26-calculator slug: two-layouts order: 40 group: Build title: Two layouts lede: A focused single-player read on mobile, a comparison view on desktop, and the styling deliberately done last. ---

Mobile gets a single focused read: one player, the numbers that matter, nothing else. Desktop gets the comparison view, because comparison is what people actually open a calculator for and it needs horizontal room. The UI was rebuilt in Tailwind once the maths had stopped moving — deliberately in that order, so styling effort was not spent on layouts the formulas were about to invalidate.
