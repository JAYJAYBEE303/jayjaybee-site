  • Chemistry — the actual applied boosts at each chemistry level, not the headline figure.
  • PlayStyles — including the plus tiers, which change behaviour rather than just adding points.
  • The underlying curves — the diminishing returns the game runs that make raw stat comparison misleading at the top of the range.((These were derived from in-game testing across several rounds of tuning, not from any published source.
