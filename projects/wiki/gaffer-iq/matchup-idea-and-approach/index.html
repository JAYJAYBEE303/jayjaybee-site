The front page

The thinking was that this was going to be the front page, giving a very detailed view into matchup predictions and allowing for a deep dive on a single fixture: both sides scored, every [[calculations|sub-metric]] shown, each counter-matchup pairing openable to name the players behind it.

More than one way in

A page whose general view gives comprehensive perspectives on upcoming matchups has high potential, so it carries multiple ways to get a good idea of the future of the season rather than one.
