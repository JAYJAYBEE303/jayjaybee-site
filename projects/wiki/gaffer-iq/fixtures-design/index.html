There were varying ways I could do this. I could provide this all on one page, similar to the [[matchup-idea-and-approach|Matchups]] page, have a layout that lays things out with subtabs, or have it as one long scrollable page. I also needed to decide whether I'd go down a similar graphic-led design — something interactive and engaging for users — or one that was more numbers-focused, showing the statistics exactly as they are.

I decided to go down the route of subtabs, with lower graphic intensity and just displaying numbers as they are. I decided this was the best direction because there is a lot of statistics to fetch and show, and with it being less prediction-based — i.e. representing a true historical statistic fetch — it was important that this page represented that. With so much information to show, I felt that subtabs were the best direction to avoid overloading the user, causing them to get lost in a sea of metrics when they may be looking for something more specific rather than the general overviews Matchups provides.
