--- layout: wiki-entry parent: gaffer-iq slug: approach order: 20 group: Design Philosophy title: Approach lede: Pull as much data as possible, process it into a new perspective, and keep the platform modular enough that the data can be parsed globally. ---

The idea was to pull as much data as possible and use that data to make accurate predictions for every player and every fixture. There is a lot of data to process, so to make this with accessibility and intuitiveness was a fundamental I've tried to stick to. The data comes from two main locations in its current phase and is processed to provide a new perspective on team and player performance. On the back end, it was important to have the platform modular so that data can easily be parsed globally across the site and to allow for a scalable design.
