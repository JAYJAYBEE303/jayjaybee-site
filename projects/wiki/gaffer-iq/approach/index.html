The idea

The idea was to pull as much data as possible and use that data to make accurate predictions for every player and every fixture. There is a lot of data to process, so to make this with accessibility and intuitiveness was a fundamental I've tried to stick to. The data comes from two main locations in its current phase and is processed to provide a new perspective on team and player performance.

The back end

On the back end, it was important to have the platform modular so that data can easily be parsed globally across the site and to allow for a scalable design.
