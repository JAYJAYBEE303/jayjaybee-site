A simple-looking interface that unlocks potential after fixtures are played, allowing a more literal view of the upcoming week's fixtures.

Before a matchup is played, information is minimal. You can see who is playing home and away, as well as the recent history of that fixture.

After a match has been played, however, its potential becomes apparent. You get a graphically appropriate breakdown of all the key events that happened during that match, in a way where you can see what side the event happened for, who it was committed by, and when. You can also see exactly who was substituted for who and at what time during the game, who started, how many minutes were played, and who was left on the bench — allowing for a comprehensive view that matches modern interpretations of the same information, in a way that balances comprehensive design and detailed breakdown.
