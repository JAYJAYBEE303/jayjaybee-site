A simple-looking interface that unlocks potential after fixtures are played, allowing a more literal view of the upcoming week's fixtures.

Before kickoff

Before a matchup is played, information is minimal. You can see who is playing home and away, as well as the recent history of that fixture.

After the match

After a match has been played, however, its potential becomes apparent. You get a graphically appropriate breakdown of all the key events that happened during that match:

  • What side the event happened for, who it was committed by, and when.
  • Exactly who was substituted for who, and at what time during the game.
  • Who started, how many minutes were played, and who was left on the bench.

This allows for a comprehensive view that matches modern interpretations of the same information, in a way that balances comprehensive design and detailed breakdown.
