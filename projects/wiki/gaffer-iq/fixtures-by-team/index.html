Users might want to see more regarding specific team performance — i.e. see exactly what their wins, draws, and losses look like across the season so far, and what they can expect to come. This was also designed with the idea of being on par with current designs for the same information from official sources, but in a way where the styling is consistent with the site.

The user can click one of the teams that appears in their future or past matchups. For example, if the user is viewing Liverpool's fixtures and results and clicks on Man Utd in this tab, it will then direct them to the [[fixtures-head-to-head|Head-to-head]] area of the Fixtures page, allowing for a more specific view into that one specific matchup.
