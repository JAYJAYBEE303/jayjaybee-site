This week only. The Dashboard is deliberately locked to the single-gameweek horizon while every other view runs on the longer one, because captaincy and bench order are not multi-week questions.

It picks a captain by highest expected points among the players actually in the squad, orders the starting XI and bench, and raises risk flags — low minutes security, a brutal band, a low-confidence score. While a gameweek is in progress it reads the live endpoint and shows points as they land.
