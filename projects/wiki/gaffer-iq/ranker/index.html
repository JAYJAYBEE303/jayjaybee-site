The bridge between team-level fixture scores and player-level decisions: a player's own form multiplied by that player's fixture composite across the horizon, producing a ranked projected-value list.

Sortable by projected value, cost per point, price or minutes security; filterable by position, price threshold, team and minutes security. Permanent Value and Cost/Pt columns sit alongside average points per gameweek, the next fixture with its rank and score, and a per-gameweek fixture strip for each player. Players are listed under their full names rather than FPL's web_name, which is compact but ambiguous across a 700-player pool — and the column sorts on the string it displays rather than on a field nobody can see.
